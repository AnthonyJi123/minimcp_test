\section{Live End-of-Turn Escalation}
\label{sec:live}
\label{sec:liveloop}
\label{sec:livecurve}

\paragraph{The streaming loop.}
The protocol is turn-based: audio streams in 1\,s chunks, but the
talker speaks only after the user's turn ends and the model's native
listen/speak head is not engaged. Per-chunk probe scores are strictly
weaker than the offline read (best chunk statistic AUC 0.764) and miss
short utterances whose critical entity lands in the final chunk. The
fix is an \textbf{end-of-turn read}: after the last audio chunk,
prefill an assistant turn containing a single space and read L22 at the
assistant-start position. This read reaches \textbf{AUC 0.887} ---
above the offline reference (0.843) --- in $\sim$30\,ms, still before
the first output token, and is the loop's only decision point;
escalation-rate thresholds are quantiles of this single score.
Speculative mid-stream prefetch was measured and dropped (an expert
answer to a partial transcript answers the full question only
7--51\% of the time; Appendix~\ref{app:livedetail}). On escalation the talker speaks a
stall phrase, its \emph{own} transcription of the audio goes to the
expert (\texttt{gpt-5.5}, reasoning effort fixed low --- higher effort
buys zero accuracy on the escalated population at double the latency),
and the returned answer is injected as context for the talker to
relay.
\label{sec:inject}
On the deployed channel the relay is clean: model wrong, correct answer
injected, comply $=1.00$ ($n{=}24$); more broadly the competence signal
also predicts relay compliance (comply 42\%$\to$75\% across gate-score
quartiles) --- a model that knows it doesn't know is willing to listen.
Conflicting-injection and framing controls: Appendix~\ref{app:livedetail}.

\paragraph{The live result.}
We ran 240 frozen test queries $\times$ four escalation tiers, each a
complete turn-based spoken session, scored on \emph{deployed-channel
answer accuracy}: correctness of the content the system delivers on
its output channel. The sweeps are text-out (a TTS-on replay changes
no gate decision, Appendix~\ref{app:livedetail}); figures abbreviate
the metric as ``heard''. Headline numbers use a pre-registered
input-side filter (21 formula-LaTeX ids + 1 broken recording removed;
spoken formulas are destroyed by any TTS render before the system
hears them). Deployed-channel accuracy climbs monotonically,
$\mathbf{42.2\% \to 52.8\% \to 59.2\% \to 63.3\%}$ at 0/14/31/57\% escalation,
every tier's gain significant (paired bootstrap;
Figure~\ref{fig:dualview}). A measured always-escalate arm closes the
curve at 66.5\% (P50 4.2\,s): the last 43 points of escalation buy
3.2 points, because past the gated tiers the binding constraint is the
\emph{transcription-uplink} channel, not selection: the same sessions
re-scored with gold-text expert answers reach 92.2\%, and the channel
costs +2.3/+6.9/+12.8 points against that counterfactual across
tiers. Against the matched-rate random reference \emph{internal to
each view} the gate wins at every arm --- in the deployed view by
7.2--9.5 points, in the channel-controlled view by 5.4--8.4
(Figure~\ref{fig:dualview}). Live arm accuracies on this pool carry
a replication-noise floor of $\pm$2--3 points, so no claim here rests on
a sub-3-point live delta. Full tables (both views, both pools), latency profiles, uplink
diagnostics, and two boundary query classes (false-premise,
real-time) are in Appendix~\ref{app:livedetail}. A talker-on replay leaves the frozen probe intact (AUC 0.871/0.856
vs.\ 0.866 headless); TTS-on, first audible audio lands p50 0.55\,s
on local turns and 22--29\,ms on escalated ones (the canned stall),
expert content at p50 6.4\,s with the stall covering the head of the
wait (Appendix~\ref{app:duplexval}, ~\ref{app:livedetail}).
Re-run end to end in the \emph{concurrent} regime (target audio
interleaved into the same KV stream mid-answer; carrier turn
teacher-forced, not the official free-running serving path), the loop
reproduces where calibration matches the regime: internally, speakable
accuracy climbs 44.5$\to$71.1\%, clears matched-rate random from the
balanced tier on ($p\le.009$) and beats always-escalate (66.5\%);
externally the quarter-scale in-regime probe clears random only in
spots (exploratory, Table~\ref{tab:conclive}).
Finally, the system now runs on the model's \emph{native full-duplex}
head --- no VAD, no barge-in harness, the gate reading at the head's
own listen$\to$speak commit --- where the recalibrated probe reaches
AUC 0.830 internally / 0.709 external mean and gated accuracy clears
matched-rate random on five of six pools
(Appendix~\ref{app:native}).
