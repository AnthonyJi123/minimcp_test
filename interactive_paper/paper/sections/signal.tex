\section{Reading Competence from Duplex Models}
\label{sec:signal}

\subsection{In distribution every readout looks fine; transfer reveals
the shortcut}
\label{sec:audit}

Table~\ref{tab:signals} compares the candidate signals on
MiniCPM-o~4.5. In distribution the final-layer probe looks healthy at
AUC 0.822 --- but that number is mostly a \emph{query-type} shortcut.
The same hidden state all but names which pool a query came from, a
pool-identity oracle alone recovers most of the probe's
in-distribution margin, and appending true pool labels to the probe's
features changes nothing, because the label is already in the
representation. The decisive test is therefore leave-one-pool-out
(LOPO) transfer, which we treat as the primary metric throughout. Under
LOPO the readout does not merely weaken: on held-out math it
\emph{inverts}, to AUC 0.372, and it scores a trap pool the model fails
100\% of the time at 0.232 --- ranking the queries it is certain to get
wrong as the safest to keep local. An external query-surface router fails the same way from the other
side --- never clearing the pool oracle in distribution, collapsing
under LOPO, barely moving with two orders of magnitude more data:
instance-level competence lives in the model's state, not on the query
surface (Appendices~\ref{app:signal},~\ref{app:router}).

Verbalized self-assessment transfers differently. Asked \emph{before}
answering, $\ptrue$ catches exactly the trap pool the probe misses,
scoring it 0.945. But timing is everything: asked \emph{after}
drafting, the model endorses its own confident-wrong answer and the
signal inverts. Post-draft $\ptrue$ is the strongest single readout in
distribution, at 0.899, and also the one a voice deadline cannot
afford --- it bills a full draft before the gate can fire.


\subsection{The damage is duplex-specific; the signal survives
mid-network}
\label{sec:duplex}

Matched pairs attribute the probe failure to the \emph{kind} of
fine-tune, not the backbone: the raw Qwen backbone, label-matched to the
duplex model's fail rates, holds LOPO math at 0.825 where the duplex
model sits at 0.372; streaming-omni and audio-only fine-tunes are
undamaged; a second duplex generation (MiniCPM-o~2.6) reproduces the
collapse (Appendix~\ref{app:signal}). A layer $\times$ position sweep
localizes it (Figure~\ref{fig:layersweep}): the collapse is specific to
(late layer $\times$ last token) on text input --- mean-pooled reads
survive at the same depth but 0.13--0.15 below the mid-layer peak
(LOPO math $\approx$0.80 here, 0.674 on MiniCPM-o~2.6), and
mid-network the last-token read recovers to \textbf{0.931 at L22},
near the raw backbone.

\emph{What breaks at the output.} \textbf{The mechanism is
unresolved}: what we can state is that duplex checkpoints exhibit a
late, distributed-read decay their backbones and non-duplex fine-tunes
do not. The natural reading --- that duplex
training re-purposes the last position for turn-control state --- we
tested rather than asserted, and it fails its own direct tests
(Appendix~\ref{app:mech}). Four candidate mechanisms fail: the cliff
does not move when the model is prefilled in speak mode, the inverting
direction is orthogonal to the model's own audio-vs-text axis, source
pools stay equally decodable at every depth in both the duplex model
and its backbone, and once features are standardized the duplex
model's representational drift from that backbone does not localize
late at all. What does separate them is how the probe's read is
\emph{carried} (Figure~\ref{fig:mech}): through mid-network the
transferable signal is distributed across directions, and over the
final layers of both duplex models --- but neither raw backbone ---
that distributed component decays and inverts, on MiniCPM-o~4.5 while
the read concentrates into a handful of dominant directions. Duplex
tuning does not delete the competence signal; it stops delivering it
to the output position the standard readout uses. Direct tests of the
turn-control reading do not support a takeover: the duplex
output layer alone stays biased toward emitting control tokens, but
the inverting direction aligns with neither the control vocabulary
nor an explicit listen/speak axis, so the decay is real and
duplex-specific while its cause --- which objective, which mechanism
--- remains open. The mid-network optimum is not a
MiniCPM quirk: on
NemotronLabs-VoiceChat-11B --- a hybrid-Mamba duplex model,
architecturally disjoint --- the same recipe peaks mid-band at L30--34
of 56 and transfers to the external pools at AUC 0.70--0.79 with a
quarter of the calibration data (Appendix~\ref{app:nvda}).

\subsection{Text-calibrated, speech-deployed --- in end-to-end models
only}
\label{sec:xmodal}

Probes trained on text hidden states and scored on speech-input hidden
states locate where the representation becomes modality-shared: early
layers are modality-specific (0.54--0.60), and from $\sim$50\% depth
text$\to$audio transfer plateaus at 0.82--0.87 (0.855 at the deployed
L22). This replicates on MiniCPM-o~2.6 and cross-family on
Qwen2.5-Omni. The decisive control is \textbf{Freeze-Omni}: identical
frozen LLM weights with audio attached by an adapter --- and
cross-modal transfer is dead (0.52--0.60). The modality-shared mid-layer
core is \emph{created by training the backbone on the modality}; the
cheap recipe ``calibrate on text, deploy on speech'' is valid for
end-to-end models and invalid for adapter-attached ones. Verbalized
introspection shows the mirror-image failure: on audio input,
pre-answer $\ptrue$ collapses on both MiniCPM duplex generations while
both non-duplex controls stay intact; controlled arms show the verbal
self-check runs over text-token pathways that audio embeddings do not
feed --- duplicating the question as text restores it --- while a
linear probe reads the same information off the same forward pass at
0.93 (Appendix~\ref{app:signal}). All audio-side findings replicate on
200 real human recordings (SD-QA), ruling out TTS artifacts.
\textbf{The mid-layer probe is the one readout that survives every
training regime that can converse}; the live system deploys the same
L22 read plus two zero-latency pooled positions (the \emph{deployed}
probe; Appendix~\ref{app:signal}).
