\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Pre-answer competence estimation.}
Text-language-model correctness can be estimated from verbal
self-evaluation \citep{kadavath2022language,lin2022teaching}, output
uncertainty, or hidden representations
\citep{azaria2023internal,burns2023discovering,marks2024geometry}.
Intermediate layers can carry more useful information than the output
distribution \citep{orgad2024llms}, and trained hidden-state probes are
strong factual-confidence estimators \citep{mahaut2024factual}.
Semantic entropy improves uncertainty estimation by comparing sampled
answers \citep{kuhn2023semantic,farquhar2024detecting}; Semantic
Entropy Probes amortize this signal into a pre-generation or post-hoc
hidden-state read \citep{kossen2024semantic}. Attention-map probes and
multimodal uncertainty fusion extend the same broad idea
\citep{chuang2024lookback,umui2026}. This work establishes that
competence is readable in text models. It does not show where the read
remains reliable after duplex speech training or whether a probe
calibrated on text transfers to speech-input states.

\paragraph{Selective routing.}
HybridLLM \citep{ding2024hybrid}, RouteLLM
\citep{ong2024routellm}, and query-side routers
\citep{hybridserve2025,hu2024routerbench,llmrouterbench2026} choose a
model for a complete text query from query features or preference
data. FrugalGPT organizes cascades that can score an earlier model's
response \citep{chen2023frugalgpt}, while AutoMix verifies a drafted
answer before escalating \citep{aggarwal2024automix}. Closest to our
signal, recent methods read pre-generation activations or fit linear
success probes on frozen states
\citep{varshney2026llmrouter,lugoloobi2025difficulty,
lugoloobi2026failures}. A second family trains the decision into the
generator: Toolformer and Self-RAG learn tool or reflection tokens
\citep{schick2023toolformer,asai2024selfrag}; Co-LLM and CITER learn
token-level deferral \citep{shen2024collaborative,zheng2025citer}; and
pause, thought, or think/no-think tokens allocate more internal
computation
\citep{goyal2024pause,zelikman2024quietstar,yang2025qwen3,
guo2025deepseekr1}. Learning-to-defer and early-exit methods make
related decisions within a model
\citep{madras2018predict,schuster2022confident}. Our contribution is
not the linear pre-generation probe by itself. We test whether this
kind of read survives duplex training and audio input, and use a
deployment-time threshold without modifying the speech checkpoint.

\paragraph{Duplex control and live handoff.}
End-to-end spoken dialogue includes native duplex models
\citep{nguyen2023generative,defossez2024moshi,zeng2024glm4voice,
bayling2026duplex}, streaming omni models
\citep{xu2025qwen25omni,openbmb2026minicpmo}, frozen-backbone adapters
\citep{wang2024freezeomni}, and audio fine-tunes
\citep{chu2024qwen2audio}. Their control interfaces decide when to
listen or speak through state, idle, synchronization, or interruption
tokens \citep{wang2024fsm,zhang2024duplex,veluri2024syncllm,
ma2024lslm,fu2024vita,yu2024salmonnomni}, structured action channels
\citep{zhang2026duplexsla}, or small classifier heads
\citep{liao2025flexduo,chen2025minmo}. MiniCPM-o~4.5 likewise predicts
a listen/speak state before content generation
\citep{openbmb2026minicpmo}. These policies answer \emph{when} the
model should speak, not whether its content is likely to be correct.

Recent systems also connect a duplex interface to a stronger resource.
MoshiRAG triggers asynchronous retrieval from the model's emerging
output and returns knowledge to the local model
\citep{chien2026moshirag}. DuplexOmni uses a trained think-control
token to hand work to a separate reasoning layer
\citep{huang2026duplexomni}. These systems demonstrate that a live
handoff is possible, but they do not evaluate a competence score read
from a frozen duplex checkpoint before generation.

\paragraph{Positioning.}
Prior work therefore establishes three ingredients separately:
competence can be read from text models, text queries can be routed,
and duplex models can coordinate speaking or external computation.
To our knowledge, the reviewed literature does not establish their
conjunction: whether a frozen duplex-trained speech model exposes a
pre-answer competence signal that survives unseen query pools and
text-to-speech transfer, and whether that signal arrives early enough
to improve live selective escalation. We evaluate these conditions
directly. The claims are about readout robustness and system utility;
the training mechanism behind the late-layer failure remains open.
