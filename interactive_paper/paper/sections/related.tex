\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Full-duplex real-time dialogue.}
End-to-end spoken dialogue spans native duplex designs --- dGSLM
\citep{nguyen2023generative}, Moshi \citep{defossez2024moshi},
GLM-4-Voice \citep{zeng2024glm4voice}, single-LLM native full-duplex
\citep{bayling2026duplex} --- streaming omni models
\citep{xu2025qwen25omni,openbmb2026minicpmo}, frozen-backbone adapters
\citep{wang2024freezeomni}, and audio fine-tunes
\citep{chu2024qwen2audio}. A parallel line adds explicit turn-taking
control: FlexDuo \citep{liao2025flexduo}, MinMo \citep{chen2025minmo},
and Freeze-Omni's state heads decide \emph{when the local model should
speak}, at 120--250\,ms cadences that show a live loop tolerates far
more decision latency than our $\sim$30\,ms read
\citep[cf.][]{sync2026turntaking,chrono2026thinking}.
Our gate decides the orthogonal question --- \emph{whether the local
model should be the one answering} --- and the head-based controllers'
shared choice of a last-layer readout is exactly the point our results
show duplex fine-tuning damages.

\paragraph{Does the model know what it knows?}
$\ptrue$ self-evaluation \citep{kadavath2022language}, verbalized
uncertainty \citep{lin2022teaching}, and truthfulness probes on hidden
states \citep{azaria2023internal,burns2023discovering,marks2024geometry}
established that correctness is readable internally; intermediate
layers carry more of it than the output distribution
\citep{orgad2024llms}. Semantic entropy
\citep{kuhn2023semantic,farquhar2024detecting} needs multiple sampled
generations; Semantic Entropy Probes \citep{kossen2024semantic}
amortize it into a hidden-state probe --- the closest signal to ours,
but read post-hoc on text-only half-duplex models. Multimodal
uncertainty fusion \citep{umui2026} asks a downstream question; we ask
\emph{where} in a duplex model a modality-robust read survives at all.

\paragraph{Selective escalation and routing.}
FrugalGPT \citep{chen2023frugalgpt}, HybridLLM \citep{ding2024hybrid},
RouteLLM \citep{ong2024routellm}, and confidence-based cascades
\citep{hybridserve2025,hu2024routerbench,llmrouterbench2026} route
from query-side features or preference data; AutoMix
\citep{aggarwal2024automix} escalates by \emph{post-answer}
self-verification, which a voice deadline forbids. Both assumptions
--- query-side features, and the large model answering the user
directly --- fail in full-duplex voice, where the session lives in
the small model (query-side ceiling quantified in \S\ref{sec:audit}).
A second family trains the decision \emph{into the weights} as
control tokens: pause and thought tokens
\citep{goyal2024pause,zelikman2024quietstar}, fused think/no-think
modes \citep{yang2025qwen3,guo2025deepseekr1}, and the built-in think
gate MiniCPM-o~4.5 itself ships \citep{openbmb2026minicpmo}. These
decide \emph{more compute}, not \emph{which model}, require
fine-tuning the talker, and bake the policy into the checkpoint ---
changing budget or expert means retraining. Our gate is the pluggable
complement: a logistic read over a frozen checkpoint, refit from
stored activations in minutes, transferring across modality and
model family (\S\ref{sec:transfer}), and it beats the model's own
trained think gate at matched budget (Table~\ref{tab:policy}).
Learning-to-defer \citep{madras2018predict} and early exit
\citep{schuster2022confident} decide inside one model.

\paragraph{The gap.}
Duplex work decides when to speak; routing work decides which model
--- from the query surface, after a first answer, or via tokens
trained into the weights; uncertainty work asks whether the model is
confident, almost always offline and in text. No prior work we know
of asks whether a full-duplex speech model \emph{already} exposes a
pre-answer, modality-robust, internal competence signal early enough
to drive a live small-to-large handoff while preserving the session.
