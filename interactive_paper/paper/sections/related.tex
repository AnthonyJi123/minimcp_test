\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Full-duplex real-time dialogue.}
End-to-end spoken dialogue spans native duplex designs --- dGSLM
\citep{nguyen2023generative}, Moshi \citep{defossez2024moshi},
GLM-4-Voice \citep{zeng2024glm4voice}, single-LLM native full-duplex
\citep{bayling2026duplex} --- streaming omni models
\citep{xu2025qwen25omni,openbmb2026minicpmo}, frozen-backbone adapters
\citep{wang2024freezeomni}, and audio fine-tunes
\citep{chu2024qwen2audio}. Inside these models the standard control
interface for turn-taking is a policy \emph{trained into the
generator}: a neural-FSM scheme has the LLM emit explicit
listen/speak transition tokens \citep{wang2024fsm}; duplex
time-slicing learns an idle token that keeps the model silent
\citep{zhang2024duplex}; SyncLLM aligns the two speech streams with
synchronization tokens \citep{veluri2024syncllm}; LSLM halts
generation on a detected-interruption token \citep{ma2024lslm}; VITA
and SALMONN-omni emit state tokens around the speak/listen decision
\citep{fu2024vita,yu2024salmonnomni}; DuplexSLA adds a third
structured-action channel so tool calls are emitted without pausing
speech \citep{zhang2026duplexsla}; BayLing-Duplex converts a
turn-based SpeechLM into a native duplex one with a handful of state
tokens and interleaved channels \citep{bayling2026duplex}; and the
checkpoint we probe is itself of this design --- MiniCPM-o~4.5
predicts a binary listen/speak token before it generates content
\citep{openbmb2026minicpmo}. A neighboring line moves the
same decision into small classifier heads --- FlexDuo
\citep{liao2025flexduo}, MinMo \citep{chen2025minmo}, Freeze-Omni's
state heads --- at 120--250\,ms cadences that show a live loop
tolerates far more decision latency than our $\sim$30\,ms read
\citep[cf.][]{sync2026turntaking,chrono2026thinking}; a further line
tunes these behaviours by RL over interaction-level rewards
\citep{ohashi2026interactivity}.
All of these decide \emph{when the local model should speak}; our
gate decides the orthogonal question --- \emph{whether the local
model should be the one answering} --- and their shared choice of a
trained last-layer readout is exactly the intervention our results
show duplex fine-tuning damages.

\paragraph{Does the model know what it knows?}
$\ptrue$ self-evaluation \citep{kadavath2022language}, verbalized
uncertainty \citep{lin2022teaching}, and truthfulness probes on hidden
states \citep{azaria2023internal,burns2023discovering,marks2024geometry}
established that correctness is readable internally; intermediate
layers carry more of it than the output distribution
\citep{orgad2024llms}. Semantic entropy
\citep{kuhn2023semantic,farquhar2024detecting} needs multiple sampled
generations; Semantic Entropy Probes \citep{kossen2024semantic}
amortize it into a hidden-state probe --- the closest signal to ours,
but read post-hoc on text-only half-duplex models. Multimodal
uncertainty fusion \citep{umui2026} asks a downstream question; we ask
\emph{where} in a duplex model a modality-robust read survives at all.

\paragraph{Selective escalation and routing.}
The classic routers are creatures of the turn-based, text-only
setting: FrugalGPT \citep{chen2023frugalgpt}, HybridLLM
\citep{ding2024hybrid}, RouteLLM \citep{ong2024routellm}, and
confidence-based cascades
\citep{hybridserve2025,hu2024routerbench,llmrouterbench2026} make one
decision per complete typed query, from query-side features or
preference data, before any model has answered; AutoMix
\citep{aggarwal2024automix} escalates by \emph{post-answer}
self-verification, which a voice deadline forbids. Both assumptions
--- query-side features, and the large model answering the user
directly --- fail in full-duplex voice, where the session lives in
the small model (query-side ceiling quantified in \S\ref{sec:audit}).
A second family trains the routing decision \emph{into the weights}
as special tokens emitted during decoding: Toolformer
\citep{schick2023toolformer} teaches the model to insert API-call
tokens by self-supervised fine-tuning; Self-RAG
\citep{asai2024selfrag} fine-tunes the full model on distilled critic
labels so reflection tokens trigger retrieval and self-critique;
Co-LLM \citep{shen2024collaborative} and CITER \citep{zheng2025citer}
learn a per-token deferral decision to a larger model; pause and
thought tokens \citep{goyal2024pause,zelikman2024quietstar}, fused
think/no-think modes \citep{yang2025qwen3,guo2025deepseekr1}, and the
built-in think gate MiniCPM-o~4.5 itself ships
\citep{openbmb2026minicpmo} route \emph{compute} the same way. The
design is uniform --- extend the vocabulary, then fine-tune the
generator until the policy lives in the checkpoint --- and so is the
cost: supervision must be constructed and the talker fine-tuned, the
policy is frozen at training time (a new expert, budget, or threshold
means retraining), and in a duplex model fine-tuning the talker is
exactly the intervention \S\ref{sec:duplex} shows damages the
last-layer readout. Our gate is the pluggable complement: a logistic
read over a frozen checkpoint, refit from stored activations in
minutes, its budget moved by a deployment-time threshold, transferring
across modality and model family (\S\ref{sec:transfer}) --- and it
beats the model's own trained think token at matched budget
(Table~\ref{tab:policy}). Learning-to-defer \citep{madras2018predict}
and early exit \citep{schuster2022confident} decide inside one model.

\paragraph{Escalating out of a live duplex session.} Two concurrent
systems do escalate from a small duplex model to a stronger resource,
and both differ from us in \emph{what fires the handoff}. MoshiRAG
\citep{chien2026moshirag} pairs a compact duplex interface with
selective retrieval, exploiting the lag between speech onset and the
first content word to fetch asynchronously and inject the result as
embeddings --- the trigger is read off the model's own emerging
\emph{output}, and what arrives is knowledge for the small model
rather than an answer from a larger one. DuplexOmni
\citep{huang2026duplexomni} separates a real-time interaction layer
from a pluggable thinking layer and trains a think-control token that
hands work to it; the policy is once more fine-tuned into the talker.
Our gate decides \emph{before} the talker commits to any output, from
a frozen checkpoint's internal state, with no talker fine-tuning and a
budget moved by a deployment-time threshold.

\paragraph{The gap.}
The prior work factors cleanly along two axes --- \{text-based,
full-duplex\} $\times$ \{policy trained in as special tokens, probe
read out of frozen internals\}. Three cells are dense: text models
both train routing tokens and read probes; duplex models train their
control policies in as tokens (or heads), including the two systems
above that escalate out of a live session. The fourth cell is empty:
no prior work we know of reads a \emph{frozen} full-duplex model's
internal state for a pre-answer, modality-robust competence signal
--- and it is empty at exactly the point our results say
token-training is least safe, since fine-tuning the talker is what
damages the readout those trained policies sit on. That empty cell is
this paper: a probe early enough to drive a live small-to-large
handoff while preserving the session.
