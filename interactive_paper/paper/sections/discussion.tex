\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What the signal detects.}
The pre-answer probe is a \emph{retrieval-failure} detector: strongest
where an empty lookup is observable, blind to false premises, and
complementary to decode-time signals, which motivates a decode-time
check behind it (Appendix~\ref{app:livedetail}).

\paragraph{Limitations.}
(i) One phrasing per $\ptrue$ variant; real-speech validation is
scripted read speech in one dialect; formula-symbolic content is out of
scope for the audio arm (any TTS render destroys it). (ii) The
second-family replication covers mid-band readability and frozen-recipe
transfer only --- not matched-pair attribution, the live loop, or the
concurrent regime. (iii) Labels come from one judge family (a stronger
re-judge bounds deltas at $\le$1 point), no human labels.
(iv) One session per query per tier ($\pm$2--3 point floor); under
concurrent prefill the frozen read degrades (AUC .87$\to$.76) and an
in-regime refit recovers .82 --- calibration must follow the regime,
and scale helps only partly (a full 2{,}310-row in-regime refit
lifts external mean AUC .64$\to$.69, still short of turn-based .77);
that concurrent loop is exploratory (teacher-forced carrier,
random-clearance internal but spotty externally;
Appendix~\ref{app:duplexval}). The deployed \emph{native} full-duplex
regime lands strictly between the two (in-regime AUC .83 internal /
.71 external mean; Appendix~\ref{app:native}), so much of the
concurrent cost was the harness read point itself; what does not carry
across any regime change is the thresholds.
(v) Layer and features were chosen on internal calibration-split
cross-validation and confirmed on held-out pools, not by nested
selection inside each held-out pool (Appendix~\ref{app:signal}).
(vi) The mechanism behind the late-layer decay is unresolved
(Appendix~\ref{app:mech}). (vii) The gate now runs and validates under the model's native
full-duplex head, with barge-in and backchannel handling measured as
the head's own behavior (Appendix~\ref{app:native}); what remains
future work is the harder content, not the channel --- disfluent
self-correction, chained tool calls mid-utterance, and multi-turn
grounding, as full-duplex agent benchmarks now target
\citep{lin2026fdbv3} --- plus one measured deployment gap: a new
question during the thinker wait derails the pending relay 7/10
times, so the in-flight expert call should be aborted on user speech.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
Duplex-trained speech models carry a competence signal their standard
readouts miss --- a mid-network read, available half a second before
the first audible word. Never gone; only misread.
